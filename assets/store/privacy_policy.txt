PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: 2026-09-03

Full policy: https://www.wardrobe.chat/privacy-policy

1. INTRODUCTION

Wardrobe Chat ("Wardrobe") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile app and website (www.wardrobe.chat).

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

- Account data: email, username, password, Google/Apple sign-in details, optional phone/email verification, profile info
- Wardrobe data: clothing images, types, colors, placement, wear history, saved outfits
- Social data: friends, messages, comments, likes, style posts, blocks, and reports
- Avatar & try-on: body photos, generated avatars, and try-on results
- AI chat & suggestions: messages and wardrobe context used for recommendations
- Device data: device info, push tokens, analytics, crash reports

3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

- Provide wardrobe, social, AI styling, avatar, and try-on features
- Authenticate users and secure accounts
- Send notifications based on your preferences
- Enforce safety (reports/blocks) and provide support
- Improve the Service and comply with law

4. STORAGE AND SECURITY

- Firebase Authentication for sign-in
- Wardrobe Chat API (Laravel / wardrobe.chat) for app data
- Secure cloud storage for images
- Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications
- Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)

5. THIRD PARTIES

- Google Firebase, Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In
- AI providers (e.g. OpenAI, Google Gemini) for suggestions, chat, avatar, try-on
- We do not sell your personal information

6. YOUR RIGHTS

- Access and edit data in the app
- Delete your account in Account Settings
- Request export: privacy@wardrobechat.com
- Control notifications and privacy settings

7. CHILDREN

Not intended for users under 13.

8. CONTACT

Privacy: privacy@wardrobechat.com
Support: support@wardrobechat.com
Website: https://www.wardrobe.chat/privacy-policy
